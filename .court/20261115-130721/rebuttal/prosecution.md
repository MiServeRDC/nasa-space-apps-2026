# Rebuttal for the Prosecution

The charge targets the claim as stated: submit and pitch *exactly as it stands*. Answering the defense in order:

1. **Survives, but narrowly.** The offline thesis is genuinely responsive on Relevance. Yet it is a thesis judges must take on faith in 30 seconds; being right about cloud invalidity does not make the rest of the system competitive. It carries the premise, not the "as-is" verdict.

2. **Weakened.** Gather is real (SHA-256-verified BIDMC). But Evaluate and Act run on models the project itself stamps ILLUSTRATIVE_UNVALIDATED. Architectural coverage of the loop is not a validated health system; the defense counts boxes the record says are not yet sound.

3. **Fails.** This is reframing a liability as an asset. The challenge's purpose is astronaut health *decisions*; Validity/Scientific Soundness is 20% of the score. A system whose physiology, risk and readiness are unvalidated *by its own admission* gets marked down on that axis regardless of how honestly the banner is worded. Honesty prevents a dishonesty penalty; it does not manufacture validation.

4. **Real but overweighted.** The depth is verifiable in the repo, but only one of five axes rewards it, and microsecond latencies and crate counts cannot be shown in 30 seconds or 7 slides. Worse, the defense *concedes* Fresh Code compliance "must be documented before submission, not assumed" — which concedes the record does not support "as-is."

**For the jury:** point 3 — whether a self-declared clinically-unvalidated system can be called globally competitive for a health-*decisions* challenge.
