### Field 1: Project Name
**ARES-Health: Autonomous Resilient Edge-native System for Astronaut Health**

### Field 2: High-Level Project Summary (Max 150 Words)
Deep-space missions to Mars impose an impassable 8-to-48-minute round-trip radio delay and two-week solar conjunction blackouts, rendering cloud telemedicine impossible. **ARES-Health** is a 100% offline, air-gapped clinical decision and health monitoring platform architected in Safe Rust (`ares-engine`) with a microgravity-optimized 3D OLED astronaut cockpit (`ares-cockpit`). Fulfilling NASA's mandate to **Gather, Evaluate, and Act**, ARES-Health fuses contactless nocturnal Doppler radar, CSA Bio-Monitor wearables, and real-time CSA CANS neutron radiation dosimetry with episodic Point-of-Care Ultrasound (POCUS). Its Dynamic Bio-Spatiotemporal Causal Graph (ADHG) models the coupled physiatric cascade linking soleus sarcopenia and Achilles tendon laxity (NASA OSD-51, ESA Myotones) to Sclerostin-driven bone loss and internal jugular vein thrombotic stasis ($\tau_w < 4.0\text{ dyn/cm}^2$, NASA-ESA Venous Haemostasis). Using Normalized Signal Temporal Logic (NQS-STL), ARES-Health predicts clinical decompensation weeks in advance and guides deterministic NASA MedOps countermeasure playbooks with hard clinical safety gates.

### Field 3: Detailed Project Description & How It Works
#### 1. The Operational Problem
In Low Earth Orbit, astronauts rely on continuous ground monitoring from Houston. During Lunar Gateway, Artemis II+, and Mars transit missions, communication delays reach up to 48 minutes round-trip, and medical evacuation is physically impossible. Existing health dashboards fail in deep space because they (a) rely on cloud APIs, (b) display biometric metrics in disconnected silos, (c) trigger false alarms during routine exercise due to static thresholds and floating-sweat sensor artifacts, and (d) offer passive charts rather than certified clinical remediation.

#### 2. How ARES-Health Solves the Challenge ("Gather, Evaluate, Act")
* **Phase 1 — GATHER (Zero-Burden Multi-Modal Telemetry & Artifact Filtering):**
  * **Contactless Nocturnal Radar:** Ceiling-mounted mmWave / Wi-Fi CSI Doppler sensing extracts resting heart rate, HRV (RMSSD), respiratory rate, and Slow-Wave N3 sleep staging without adhesive skin electrodes, eliminating contact dermatitis while suppressing ECLSS fan harmonics ($60/120\text{ Hz}$).
  * **Active Wearable & Habitat Avionics Fusion:** Integrates continuous exercise/EVA streams modeled on **CSA Bio-Monitor (Astroskin)** and **NASA Artemis II ARCHeR**, habitat $ppCO_2$, and continuous **CSA CANS (Canadian Active Neutron Spectrometer)** real-time neutron + charged-particle dosimetry (capturing the critical 30% neutron radiation component).
  * **Spectral Entropy Signal Quality Index (SQI):** Filters out impedance spikes caused by floating sweat droplets or vigorous exercise (`SQI < 0.70`), preventing false cardiac arrhythmia alarms.

* **Phase 2 — EVALUATE (Dynamic Causal Graph, Bayesian POCUS Observer & NQS-STL):**
  * **The Physiatric Muscle-Bone-Vascular Causal Graph (ADHG):** Grounded in NASA Flight Surgeon research (Dr. Auñón-Chancellor, Dr. Barratt, Dr. Polk), **NASA-ESA Venous Haemostasis**, **NASA OSDR Federated Archives (`cgene,alsda,esa`: OSD-51, OSD-195, OSD-570/571, OSD-818)**, **NASA Life Sciences Portal (`NLSP` / `NASA-0000100`: `BRSMFSR`, `BRSMPT`)**, **NASA Lifetime Surveillance of Astronaut Health (`LSAH`)**, **ESA Myotones**, and the clinical biomechanics research of Clinical Advisor **Prof. Dr. Francesco Pegreffi** (University of Enna Kore / Rizzoli Orthopedic Institute alumnus), ARES-Health models the human body as a coupled differential system:
    1. *Sarcopenia & Muscle Quality ($\text{MQI}$):* Combines POCUS soleus muscle thickness ($\text{MT}$), fascicle pennation angle ($\theta_{\text{penn}}$), and grayscale echo-intensity ($\text{EI}$, quantifying myosteatosis) with **ESA Myotones** Achilles tendon stiffness ($\text{N/m}$) and **NLSP `BRSMFSR`** functional stretch reflex latency.
    2. *Osteopenia, LSAH Fracture Risk & Renal Stone Cascade:* Models how sub-threshold mechanical strain ($\varepsilon_{\text{ARED}} < 1000\ \mu\varepsilon$ Frost Mechanostat under **NASA Zero T2** protocols and **NASA-STD-3001 Vol. 1 `[V1 4001]`**) and depleted muscle **Irisin** upregulate osteocyte **Sclerostin (SOST)** and the **$\text{CTX-I/P1NP}$** ratio, driving calcaneus/femoral bone resorption, incomplete longitudinal DXA recovery (**LSAH** priors), and hypercalciuria ($\text{Ca/Citrate} > 0.65$).
    3. *Jugular Venous Stasis & Thrombogenesis:* Models how soleus atrophy collapses peripheral crural venous capacitance and depletes endothelial-protective exosomal **miR-126-3p**, over-distending the Internal Jugular Vein ($\text{IJV\_CSA} > 2.5\text{ cm}^2$) and dropping dimensionally exact wall shear stress $\tau_w = 2\mu v_{\max}\sqrt{\pi/\text{IJV\_CSA}}$ down to $0.31\text{ dyn/cm}^2$ ($\ll 4.0\text{ dyn/cm}^2$ critical threshold), triggering Weibel-Palade vWF/P-selectin release.
  * **Two-Stage Bayesian POCUS State Observer:** Continuously predicts latent ultrasound states ($v_{\max}$, $\text{ONSD}$, $\text{MQI}$) between weekly scans using passive telemetry and **LSAH** epidemiological priors, expanding uncertainty bounds $\pm \sigma(t)$ until a 2-minute crew POCUS verification contracts $\sigma(t) \to 0$ (satisfying **NASA-STD-3001 Vol. 1 `[V1 3003]` & `[V1 3006]`**).
  * **Normalized Quantitative Semantics Signal Temporal Logic (NQS-STL):** Non-dimensionalizes all physiological predicates onto $(-\infty, +1.0]$ to eliminate unit scale dominance, evaluates rolling temporal operators in $O(1)$ amortized time (satisfying **NASA-STD-3001 Vol. 2 `[V2 6001]`** environmental trend analysis), and extracts 7-day EWMA-OLS derivatives to compute exact **Time-To-Violation (`TTV`)** countdowns weeks before acute pathology.

* **Phase 3 — ACT (Deterministic NASA MedOps Playbooks with Hard Clinical Safety Gates):**
  * **Playbook #04 (Venous Stasis & Musculoskeletal Countermeasure DAG):**
    * *Hard Safety Gate 1 (Anticoagulation Restriction):* Strictly prohibits empirical anticoagulants (Apixaban/Enoxaparin) in isolated venous stasis without a POCUS-confirmed non-compressible thrombus, eliminating fatal traumatic intracranial hemorrhage risk during EVA.
    * *Hard Safety Gate 2 (LBNP Anti-Syncope Guardrail):* Prescribes $-30\text{ mmHg}$ Lower Body Negative Pressure (LBNP) for $45\text{ min/day}$ (or **NASA Thigh Cuff** sequestration) coupled with rhythmic calf contractions, calibrated on **NLSP Neuroendocrine Tilt** orthostatic tolerance data to automatically vent the chamber to $0\text{ mmHg}$ if systolic BP drops by $> 15\text{ mmHg}$ or HR exceeds $115\text{ bpm}$.
    * *Mechanotransductive & Nutritional Reset:* Recalibrates ARED/Zero-T2 eccentric loading ($+15\%$), administers Leucine + Vitamin D3 (Pegreffi prehabilitation model, **NASA-STD-3001 `[V1 3002]`**), and prescribes Potassium Citrate ($30\text{ mEq}$) with $500\text{ mL}$ hydration (**NASA-STD-3001 `[V2 6026]`**) for renal alkalinization.
  * **Playbook #05 (Martian $0.38g$ Surface Landing Gait Stability):** Computes the **Gait Asymmetry Index ($\text{GAI}$)** and Dynamic Knee Valgus angle from EVA suit IMUs upon landing (Pegreffi, *EJAP* 2025; **NLSP `BRSMFSR`** stretch reflex), tuning suit ankle/knee exoskeletal stiffness by $+25\%$ to prevent falls and Achilles tendon rupture.
